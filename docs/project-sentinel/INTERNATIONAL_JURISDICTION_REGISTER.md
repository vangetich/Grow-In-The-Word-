# Grow In The Word — International Jurisdiction Assessment Register

**Status:** Starter register; no jurisdiction is approved for launch by this document  
**Owner:** Product owner to assign legal, privacy and safeguarding reviewers  
**Companion:** PHASE_00D_MASTER_INTERNATIONAL_GOVERNANCE_BLUEPRINT.md

## 1. Purpose

Track jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction research for child protection, privacy, consent, online safety, international transfers, consumer obligations and copyright. This is a work register, not a legal opinion. A country must not be treated as launch-approved merely because it appears below.

## 2. Required fields for each jurisdiction

Each completed profile must record:

- Jurisdiction and territorial scope.
- Intended service entity and target user groups.
- Processing activities and data categories.
- Applicable legislation, regulations, commencement dates and amendments.
- Official regulator guidance and codes.
- Child definition and age-related rules relevant to each purpose.
- Age assurance and parental/guardian authorization requirements.
- Child assent, transparency and best-interests requirements where applicable.
- Lawful bases and sensitive-data requirements.
- Rights handling, retention and deletion.
- Safeguarding, online safety and reporting duties.
- Advertising, profiling and consumer restrictions.
- Data localization and international transfer requirements.
- Processor and breach-notification obligations.
- Copyright and content-distribution issues.
- Required technical and operational controls.
- Legal questions and uncertainties.
- Authoritative sources and retrieval/review date.
- Local counsel reviewer, advice date and scope.
- Product owner approval, restrictions and next review date.

## 5. Regional sequencing proposal

A possible research sequence, subject to owner approval and market strategy:

1. Kenya — establish the first complete reference profile.
2. Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda — assess East African expansion and differences.
3. South Africa — assess a distinct African regulatory and operational environment.
4. United Kingdom and European Union — assess child-design, privacy and transfer requirements.
5. United States, Canada and Australia — assess their separate child privacy and online safety regimes.

Sequence is a planning suggestion, not an assumption about commercial priority or legal similarity.

## 6. Research quality rules

- Prefer official legislation portals, regulators and court/authority publications.
- Record exact section/article and version, not just a homepage.
- Record publication, commencement and amendment dates.
- Separate binding law from regulator guidance, codes, industry practice and recommendations.
- Mark interpretation questions explicitly; do not convert uncertain interpretation into a product rule.
- Obtain local legal advice for material uncertainties and launch decisions.
- Recheck sources before implementation and again before launch.
- Keep a change history and identify impacted requirements, tests, data flows and markets.

## 7. Jurisdiction profile status

Use only:
- Not assessed.
- Research in progress.
- Counsel review required.
- Controls specified.
- Implementation verified.
- Approved for limited launch.
- Approved for launch.
- Suspended.

A status change must include reviewer, date, evidence and scope. "Approved for launch" applies only to the reviewed service, features, data uses, territories and operational setup.
